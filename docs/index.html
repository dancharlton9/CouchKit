Your gaming PC,
as a console.

CouchKit turns a Windows PC running Playnite into a proper living-room console. It adds an Xbox-style guide, Discord parties, achievements, and lighting that follows your game, all from the controller, with no keyboard or mouse needed.

Double-tap the Xbox button. There's the Guide.

A console-style guide slides in over whatever you're playing, with no alt-tabbing and no desktop. Switch tabs with LB and RB, move with the D-pad or stick, and press A to select.

Pop-ups that don't steal focus.

Calls, messages and achievements appear over the game without taking the controller away from it. They're big enough to read from the sofa.

Everything a console does, on your PC.

Every feature is optional and switches itself off if the app it needs isn't installed. Set it all up from Playnite's add-on settings, without editing config files.

The Xbox button, fixed

Tap it to flip between the game and Playnite. Hold Back + Start to close a game, and keep holding to force-close it.

Always on the TV

Playnite and games stay on the TV, even after streaming with Sunshine, Apollo or Moonlight. Sound switches to your headset when it's on and back to the TV when it's off.

Discord parties

Call friends, join voice channels, read and reply to messages, and mute or deafen, all from the Guide. It uses Discord's own mute, so in-game chat keeps working. English Discord

Achievements

Progress on the now-playing card, a full list for the game you're playing, your recent unlocks across every game, and a proper TV pop-up when you unlock one.

Lighting that plays along

Smooth, animated gradients on every RGB device OpenRGB can drive. While you play, the colours come from the game's artwork.

Smart home

Home Assistant scenes and room lights from the Guide. Your Nanoleaf panels and room lights can follow the game's colours, then go back to normal afterwards.

Audio mixer

Choose where sound plays and balance the game against Discord. Turn the game down without your friends getting quieter.

PC status

CPU and GPU temperatures and load, memory, uptime and drive space at a glance, plus screen off, restart and shut down from the sofa.

Notifications & screenshots

Missed calls, messages, achievements and low controller battery are all kept in one place. Take a screenshot from the Guide without leaving the game.

Not using an Xbox pad? It notices.

CouchKit recognises PlayStation and Switch controllers used through Steam Input or DS4Windows, and the Guide speaks their language.

Up and running in two minutes.

CouchKit is a Playnite add-on. It installs, starts and updates itself with Playnite.

Download

Get the latest CouchKit-x.y.z.pext from Releases and double-click it. Playnite asks to install it.

Set it up

In Playnite's desktop mode, open Add-ons → Extension settings → CouchKit. Pick your TV and headset, and switch on what you want.

Grab the controller

Switch Playnite to fullscreen mode and double-tap the Xbox button. System → CouchKit status shows what's working.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 and Playnite 10, used in fullscreen mode. Optional: an Xbox (or translated) controller · OpenRGB for lighting · Discord · PlayniteAchievements and HowLongToBeat add-ons · Home Assistant or Nanoleaf · FanControl for CPU temperature · Sunshine, Apollo or Moonlight for streaming.
